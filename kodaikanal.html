Welcome to kodaikanal

Kodaikanal, translationally meaning "The Gift of the Forest" in TamilNadu.
It is a breathtaking hill station nestled at an altitude of 2,225 meters (7,300 feet) in the Palani Hills of the Western Ghats. It is located in the Dindigul district of Tamil Nadu, India.
Frequently crowned as the "Princess of Hill Stations," It was originally established in 1845 by American Christian missionaries and British bureaucrats as a cool sanctuary to escape the sweltering heat and tropical diseases of the southern plains.

Travel Ideas 

  Morning   : Arrive in Kodaikanal, check into your hotel, and visit Bryant Park for a walk among colorful flowers.
  Afternoon : Head to Kodaikanal Lake for boating or cycling around the water.
  Evening   : Walk along  Coaker's Walk   to watch the sunset and the evening mist roll over the hills.
